# iPhone Review Device Policy

## Purpose

The iPhone can be used for review, planning notes, and approval reminders. It
is not an execution controller in the current plan.

## Allowed

- reading roadmap docs.
- reviewing changelog and matrices.
- writing human notes.
- preparing approval wording.

## Forbidden

- WSL, Hermes, wrapper, RunPod, or robot control.
- storing raw local-only values.
- acting as an always-on control device.
- approving broad or implicit GO.

## Approval Boundary

Any future control role requires separate explicit approval and しずめ review.

この範囲では問題を検出していません。
